Dia

Dia is a simple notes, tasks, and calendar app for iOS and macOS.

Your files
Store them locally or sync through your own iCloud Drive folder.
No account
No advertising, tracking, analytics, or developer-operated cloud.
Notes
Autosave, links, pinning, archives, sharing, and export.
Tasks
Quick scheduling, repeating reminders, reordering, and actionable notifications.
Agenda
Calendar events and scheduled tasks in one view.
Calendars
Apple and Google calendars, Reminders, guest invitations, event creation, calls, and directions.
Personalization
40 themes, custom colors, fonts, and typography controls.
